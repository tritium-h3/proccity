# Blocks Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** a fourth stage, `blocks`, that divides every developed superblock into zoned blocks with
collectors and local roads, by recursive cuts traced along a terrain-following field.

**Architecture:** pure modules under `src/stages/blocks/` (rules, height raster, field, cut
geometry, edge classification, the recursion, zones), assembled in `stage.ts` and registered in
`STAGES`; a renderer in `src/scene/renderers/blocks.ts` that bakes blocks and streets into the
terrain tint texture and reuses the arterial ribbons.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript (strict, TS 7), Vitest, clipper2-ts via `grammar/clipper.ts`, Three.js
(`three/webgpu`) for the renderer only.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-25-blocks-design.md` (zones, district table and facing:
`docs/notes/zoningBlocks.md`).

## Global Constraints

- Stages divide, never add: a superblock's children (street strips + blocks) partition it
  (`checkPartition`, tolerance as districts use: 1 mm per metre of outline).
- Pure stage code: no DOM, no Three.js in `src/stages/`. Only `grammar/clipper.ts` imports clipper.
- No layout distance hard-coded in metres except physical ones (road widths, grades); block and
  collector sizes are settings.
- Zones, district zone table, facing table and use weights live in `stages/blocks/rules.ts`,
  copied from the notes (Financial: UO, UCO, UCOR; Urban weights = Low urban's).
- Residential zones never face an arterial (facing table: MR, UR, HR, LR face L only).
- Undeveloped districts' superblocks are left untouched (no children).
- Dead ends: 0, apart from Suburban cul-de-sacs (counted separately).
- Every road accepted: no turn over 120° (raw and simplified as laid), no self-crossing.
- Comments explain *why*. Docs (CLAUDE.md) ride along with the change.
- Git: branch `feat/blocks`, a commit per task, merge to main when green, restart the service.

## Review Focus

1. A superblock whose boundary has a single road side (against water or an Undeveloped district):
   it must still produce valid blocks or stay one block, never a road ending in open land.
   (Task 4 test: a superblock with roads on one side only.)
2. A tiny or sliver superblock (the arterial stage leaves some): it becomes one block or stays
   unzoned, never throws or produces zero-area children. (Task 3 test.)
3. Steep ground in a dense district: cuts must turn towards the contours rather than break the
   local Max grade. (Task 2 test on a tilted plane.)
4. A zone pin whose block regenerates into a district or road situation that forbids the zone: the
   pin is dropped with a reason, not applied. (Task 5 test.)
5. 30 km still generates (slower) without timeouts in tests. (Task 1 measurement, Task 6 test.)

**Interfaces:**
- Produces (`rules.ts`):
  - `type Zone = 'LR'|'LC'|'LO'|'HR'|'HC'|'HO'|'HCR'|'HCO'|'MR'|'MC'|'MO'|'MCR'|'UR'|'UO'|'UCR'|'UCO'|'UCOR'`, `ZONES: Zone[]`
  - `type Category = 'A'|'C'|'L'`; `FACES: Record<Zone, Category[]>` (the notes' adjacency table)
  - `DISTRICT_ZONES: Record<Exclude<DistrictType,'undeveloped'>, Zone[]>` (the notes' table, Financial corrected)
  - `type Use = 'R'|'C'|'O'`; `USES: Record<Zone, Use[]>` (LR→R, HCR→C,R, UCOR→C,O,R, …)
  - `USE_WEIGHTS: Record<DistrictType, Record<Use, number>>` (spec table; Urban = Low urban; undeveloped all 0)
  - `zoneWeight(t: DistrictType, z: Zone): number` = mean of `USE_WEIGHTS[t][u]` over `USES[z]`
  - `type Family = 'sparse'|'dense'`; `familyOf(t: DistrictType): Family` (Suburban, Rural → sparse)
  - `MIN_LOT: Record<'L'|'H'|'M'|'U', { frontage: number; depth: number }>` provisional: L 20×35, H 8×25, M 30×40, U 30×30 (metres); `densityOf(z: Zone)` from the first letter
- Produces (`geometry.ts`, pure):
  - `obb(mp: MultiPolygon): { centre: Point2; axis: number /*radians, long side*/; long: number; short: number }` (minimum-area rectangle of the hull, rotating calipers)
  - `thickness(mp: MultiPolygon): number` = 2·area/perimeter
  - `splitBy(piece: MultiPolygon, line: Point2[], width: number): [MultiPolygon, MultiPolygon, MultiPolygon] | null` — (strip, side A, side B): strip = band of `width` along `line` ∩ piece; the rest split into exactly two parts by the line (null if the line doesn't cross the piece into two parts, or either part is empty)
- Produces (`divide.ts`): `divideSuperblock(sb: MultiPolygon, ctx: DivideCtx): { streets: Street[]; blocks: MultiPolygon[] }` with
  `interface Street { points: Point2[]; category: 'C'|'L'; width: number; culDeSac?: boolean }` and
  `interface DivideCtx { family: Family; s: BlockSettings; minBlockArea: number; rng: () => number; roadAt(p: Point2): Category | null; cutter: Cutter }`,
  `type Cutter = (piece: MultiPolygon, ctx: DivideCtx) => { line: Point2[] }[]` (candidate cut lines, best first). Task 1's cutter: straight lines through the OBB centre across the long axis, offsets 0, ±10%, ±20% of the long side.
- Produces (`stage.ts`): `blocks: Stage` with settings (keys, defaults): `collectorWidth` 18 m, `localWidth` 12 m, `denseDepth` 70 m, `denseLength` 160 m, `sparseDepth` 120 m, `sparseLength` 400 m, `denseCollectors` 400 m, `sparseCollectors` 700 m, `localGrade` 10 %, `culDeSacs` 0.05. Children per superblock: `collector:n` / `local:n` strips (attrs `road`, `width`, `category`) and `block:n` (attrs `district` (type), `faces` (e.g. `'A,L'`), `zone` (string or null)). Site data: `streets: { id: string; points: Point2[]; category: 'C'|'L'; width: number; culDeSac?: boolean }[]` and `blockStats: Record<string, number>` (new `RegionData` keys, validated like `roads`/`stats`).

### Task 2: Height raster and the direction field; traced cuts

**Files:**
- Create: `src/stages/blocks/height.ts`, `src/stages/blocks/field.ts`, `src/stages/blocks/trace.ts` + tests
- Modify: `src/stages/blocks/divide.ts` (traced cutter first, straight as fallback), `stage.ts`

**Interfaces:**
- Consumes: `obb`, `splitBy`, `DivideCtx` (Task 1).
- Produces:
  - `heightRaster(tree: RegionNode, cell: number): { at(x: number, z: number): number; slope(x: number, z: number): Point2 /*gradient*/ }` — built once per stage by rasterising each terrain region's `ground` triangles (barycentric fill), not by `groundHeightAt` per sample (a linear scan; far too slow). `cell = siteSize / 500`.
  - `crossField(h, edges: Point2[][], family: Family, maxGrade: number): (p: Point2) => number` — the angle (mod π/2) of a cross field: blend of the nearest road edge's direction and the contour direction, weighted by slope. Sparse: contour weight ~1 wherever slope > 1 %. Dense: edge direction, turned towards the contour only as far as needed to keep a road along either cross direction under `maxGrade`.
  - `traceCut(piece: MultiPolygon, field, seed: Point2, family: 0|1 /*which cross direction*/, step: number): Point2[] | null` — streamline both ways from `seed` until it leaves the piece (clipped to the boundary), null if it loops back or exceeds 3× the piece's OBB long side.
- [ ] **Step 1: Failing tests.** `height.test.ts`: a tilted plane surface (z·0.1) gives `at` within 0.5 m and `slope` ≈ (0, 0.1). `field.test.ts`: on a 15 % plane, dense field direction keeps the grade along at least one cross direction ≤ `maxGrade` (10 %); on flat ground it aligns with the nearest edge within 1°; sparse on a 5 % plane aligns with the contour within 5°. `trace.test.ts`: in `rect(0,0,400,200)` with a uniform field the trace from the centre is a straight line spanning the short side, ends on the boundary.
- [ ] **Step 2: Run, verify FAIL; implement; run, verify PASS.**
- [ ] **Step 3: Use traced cuts in `divide.ts`.** Candidates: seeds at the OBB centre and ±10/20 % along the long axis, both cross families, scored by: area balance (closest to half), sparse bias towards the contour family (score ×0.7 for the other), and validity (ends on roads via `ctx.roadAt`, no turn > 120° raw or simplified at 0.6 × cell, no self-crossing, grade ≤ `localGrade` sampled every 10 m). Straight candidates follow if no traced one is valid.
- [ ] **Step 4: Stage test addition:** at the 10 km defaults seeds 1–2, no street has a turn > 120° or crosses itself; ≥ 90 % of street length in sparse districts is within 15° of the contour where slope > 3 %.
- [ ] **Step 5: Full suite, typecheck, lint; commit** `feat(blocks): terrain-following traced cuts`.
