
# Zoning categories


## Block zones

Zoning is block-level. A zone is a category of a density and a use; only certain uses are valid for certain densities, so the actual number of valid zones is smaller than the potential product of densities and uses. 

Uses specify what the building can be used for, which directs the architecture. The recognized uses are:

* Residential. For housing.
* Commercial. For performing in-person commercial transactions to the general public.
* Office. For general non-public-customer-serving business.
* Mixed. A building with two or more uses. The building is designed to perform these uses, and that is reflected in the architecture. Not all combinations are recognized.

Densities specify both lot size and building size and type. The densities are:

* Low density: large lots, building sizes in proportion to single-family-occupancy homes.
* High density: small lots, building sizes comparable to low density. May include attached homes.
* Midrise density: large lots, building sizes 2-5 storeys.
* Urban core density: large lots, building sizes 6+ storeys. Often a mix of uses.

The full list of zones is:

* *LR* low density residential. Ex: single-family homes.
* *LC* low density commercial. Ex: small stores with ample parking.
* *LO* low density office. Ex: small offices with ample parking.
* *HR* kigh density residential. Ex: small-lot single-occupancy houses, duplexes+, townhomes.
* *HC* high density commercial. Ex: small stores with street parking.
* *HO* high density office. Ex: small offices with street parking.
* *HCR* high density mixed commercial/residential. Ex: small stores with apartments.
* *HCO* high density mixed commerical/office. Ex: small stores with office.
* *MR* midrise residential. Ex: apartment buildings.
* *MC* midrise commerical. Ex: department stores, malls, strip malls.
* *MO* midrise office. Ex: multi-office buildings, office parks.
* *MCR* midrise mixed commercial/residential. Ex: residential complexes with integrated commerical.
* *UR* urban core residential. Ex: residential tower.
* *UO* urban core office. Ex: office tower.
* *UCR* urban core commercial/residential mixed use. Ex: residential towers with ground-floor commercial.
* *UCO* urban core commercial/office mixed use. Ex: office towers with ground-floor commercial.
* *UCOR* urban core commercial/office/residential mixed use. Ex: multi-use complexes.

## Zoning districts

The city is divided into districts, each of which is of a district type. Blocks within a zoning district must be of a type valid for that type. The recognized district types are:

* *Rural*: large lots, large centralized commercial. Valid zoning types: LR, LC, LO, MC, MO
* *Suburban*: large lots, residential. Valid zoning types: LR, LC, LO
* *Town core*: dense lots, commercial. Valid zoning types: LR, HR, HC, HO, HCR, HCO
* *Town business district*: growing density. Valid zoning types: HR, HC, HO, HCR, HCO, MR, MC, MO, MCR
* *Low urban*: high density and mid-height. Valid zoning types: MR, MC, MCR
* *Urban*: high density and uncapped height. Valid zoning types: MR, MC, MCR, UR, UCR, UCO 
* *Financial district*: high density, uncapped height, office focus. Valid zoning types: UO, UCR, UCOR
